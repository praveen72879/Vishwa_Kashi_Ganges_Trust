About The Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is dedicated to Annadan Seva — the sacred act of feeding the hungry in Varanasi. Every day thousands of devotees visit Kashi seeking blessings of Mahadev.

Our mission is to ensure no devotee and no needy person sleeps hungry in the holy land of Kashi.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide free and dignified meals to pilgrims and underprivileged individuals through transparent community support.

Our Vision

To establish a sustainable Annadan system where every needy person receives at least one free meal daily.
