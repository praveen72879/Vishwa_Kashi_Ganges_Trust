About The Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a community-driven charitable initiative based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Established with the purpose of Annadan Seva, the trust operates daily food distribution programs for pilgrims visiting Kashi Vishwanath and underprivileged families residing in and around the city.

Kashi is one of the most sacred cities in India. Thousands of devotees travel here daily. Many arrive with limited financial resources. Simultaneously, several local families struggle to meet basic nutritional needs.

The trust operates on the principle of Seva (service), Dharma (righteous action), and Sanskar (cultural values).

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide free, hygienic, and dignified meals to pilgrims, elderly individuals, daily wage workers, and economically weaker families in Varanasi through structured and transparent community support.

Our Vision

To establish a sustainable Annadan model in Kashi where every devotee and every needy person has access to at least one nutritious meal per day without discrimination.

Our Operational Model

Meal Preparation

Food is prepared under hygienic conditions with structured procurement of raw materials and monitored cooking standards.

Distribution System

Meals are distributed at identified locations including pilgrim areas, temple surroundings, and economically vulnerable neighborhoods.

Community Participation

Individuals sponsor meals on birthdays, anniversaries, religious events, and remembrance days.

Impact & Outreach

Through consistent operations, the trust has built a reliable support system for both visiting pilgrims and local residents. Donations are directly allocated toward food materials, cooking expenses, volunteer coordination, and distribution logistics.
