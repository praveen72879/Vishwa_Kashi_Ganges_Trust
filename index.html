About Our Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a humanitarian initiative based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Our trust is dedicated to Annadan Seva — the sacred act of feeding the hungry in the holy land of Kashi.

Every day thousands of devotees travel to seek blessings of Mahadev. Many pilgrims and local families struggle with food. Our mission ensures no one sleeps hungry in Kashi.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to provide free, hygienic and dignified meals to pilgrims, poor families, elderly individuals, and daily wage workers in Varanasi through transparent and community-supported donations.

Our vision is to build a sustainable Annadan system in Kashi where every devotee and every needy person has access to at least one nutritious meal every day.
