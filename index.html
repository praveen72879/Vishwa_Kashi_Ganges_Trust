About Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a charitable initiative based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The trust is established with the purpose of serving humanity through Annadan Seva (food donation).

Every day, thousands of devotees travel to Kashi to seek blessings of Mahadev. Many pilgrims come from different states with limited financial support. At the same time, several local families in Varanasi struggle to afford basic meals.

Our trust works to ensure that no devotee and no needy person sleeps hungry in the holy land of Kashi.

Our work is rooted in the values of Seva (service), Dharma (righteousness), and Sanskar (cultural values).

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide free and dignified meals to pilgrims and underprivileged people in Varanasi through transparent and community-supported charitable efforts.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable Annadan system in Kashi where every visitor and every needy person has access to at least one free meal daily.

What We Do

Annadan Seva (Free Food Distribution)

We organize regular free meal distribution programs in Varanasi for devotees visiting Kashi Vishwanath, travelers who cannot afford meals, and poor and homeless individuals. Food is prepared hygienically and distributed with respect and dignity.

Support for Devotees & Community Participation

Many pilgrims travel long distances for darshan. We aim to support them with basic food assistance during their spiritual journey.

We encourage individuals and families to sponsor meals on birthdays, anniversaries, religious occasions, and remembrance days. Donations directly support food preparation and distribution.

Why Annadan in Kashi?

Kashi is one of the most sacred cities in India. Devotees visit throughout the year seeking spiritual blessings. Providing food in this holy land is considered an act of great virtue.

Annadan is not just charity. It is service to humanity and devotion to Mahadev.

Impact Overview

  • Daily meal support for visiting pilgrims
  • Food assistance for poor families in Varanasi
  • Community-driven donation support system
  • Transparent use of funds for food service
