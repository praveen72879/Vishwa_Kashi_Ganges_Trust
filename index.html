About The Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a charitable initiative based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, dedicated to Annadan Seva — the sacred act of feeding the hungry.

Thousands of devotees visit Kashi every day seeking blessings of Mahadev. Many pilgrims travel long distances with limited resources. At the same time, several local families struggle to afford daily meals.

Our trust works to ensure that no devotee and no needy person sleeps hungry in the holy land of Kashi.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide free and dignified meals to pilgrims and underprivileged people in Varanasi through transparent and community-supported charitable efforts.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable Annadan system in Kashi where every visitor and every needy person has access to at least one free meal daily.

What We Do

Annadan Seva

Regular free meal distribution for devotees visiting Kashi Vishwanath, daily wage workers, elderly individuals, and homeless people.

Community Participation

Individuals sponsor meals on birthdays, anniversaries, religious occasions, and remembrance days. Donations directly fund food preparation and distribution.

Why Annadan in Kashi?

Kashi is one of the most sacred cities in India. Providing food here is considered an act of great virtue.

Annadan is not just charity. It is service to humanity and devotion to Mahadev.
