About Us

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, dedicated to Annadan Seva — the sacred act of feeding the hungry.

Thousands of devotees visit Kashi daily seeking blessings of Mahadev. Many pilgrims and local families struggle with food affordability. Our trust ensures no one sleeps hungry in this holy city.
