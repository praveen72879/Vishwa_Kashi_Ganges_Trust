About The Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a registered charitable organization based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Established with the vision of serving humanity through Annadan Seva, the trust focuses on providing free and dignified meals to pilgrims, daily wage workers, elderly individuals, and underprivileged families.

Kashi is one of the most sacred cities in India. Thousands of devotees travel here daily seeking spiritual blessings. Many struggle with food access during their visit. Our trust ensures that every meal served is hygienic, respectful, and prepared with devotion.

We operate with transparency, community support, and a sustainable distribution model.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide free and dignified meals to pilgrims and underprivileged individuals through community-driven charitable efforts.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable Annadan system in Kashi where every visitor and every needy person has access to at least one free meal daily.

Core Values

Seva (Service), Dharma (Righteousness), and Sanskar (Cultural Values).
