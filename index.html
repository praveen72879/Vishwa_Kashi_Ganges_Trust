About The Trust

Vishwa Kashi Ganges Charitable Trust is a registered charitable organization located at D-28/174, Pandey Haveli, Varanasi – 221001, Uttar Pradesh.

Registration Number: 20/2026VNS – Vishwanath Boni.

Established with the sacred objective of Annadan Seva, the trust focuses on providing hygienic and dignified meals to pilgrims and economically weaker sections of society.

Kashi is one of the holiest cities in India, attracting thousands of devotees daily. Many struggle for food access during their visit. Our trust ensures systematic food distribution throughout the year.

All donations are utilized transparently for food materials, cooking arrangements, distribution logistics, and volunteer support.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide free, nutritious, and respectful meals to pilgrims and underprivileged individuals in Kashi through structured and community-supported programs.

Vision: To build a sustainable Annadan ecosystem ensuring no one in the holy city sleeps hungry.

Core Values: Seva, Dharma, Sanskar, Transparency, Accountability.
